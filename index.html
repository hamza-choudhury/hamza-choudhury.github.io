It analyses 60+ assets across equities, bonds, forex, commodities and crypto, calculating spreads, volume ratios and simulated depth, then forecasts spread movements using machine learning and simulates portfolio stress scenarios. Value Proposition Delivers executable market intelligence through systematic liquidity analysis. Transforms raw data into quantifiable liquidity signals that create actionable execution advantages. Technical Skills Demonstrates end to end financial data engineering and predictive modeling. Validates proficiency in building analytical pipelines from data ingestion to dashboard deployment.

  • Multi-Asset Analysis: 60+ assets across 8 categories (Equities, Bonds, Commodities, Forex, Crypto)

  • Data Pipeline: Automated processing via yFinance API with error handling

  • Yield Curve Monitoring: Treasury yield analysis with recession signalling
  • Stress Testing Engine: Portfolio impact simulation for +50bps rate shocks

📈 ETF Cost Intelligence Engine

Project Overview A competitive intelligence engine comparing the S&P 500 UCITS ETF across six of Europe's largest providers (BlackRock, State Street, Vanguard, Xtrackers, Amundi, Invesco). It aggregates and ranks ongoing charges, fund scale, holdings count, and replication methods to reveal the true cost of passive investing. Value Proposition Delivers actionable product intelligence for ETF sales and capital markets desks. Transforms scattered regulatory data into a unified competitive landscape, highlighting exactly where cost leadership and scale leadership diverge. Technical Skills Demonstrates deep ETF product knowledge (share class selection, physical vs synthetic replication) combined with structured data engineering to build a verifiable, self-contained reporting tool.
🔧 Complete Feature Set:
  • Six-Provider Comparison: Deep-dive analysis of the S&P 500 UCITS ETF across top European issuers
  • Cost and Scale Metrics: Extraction and ranking of TER (basis points), AUM, holdings, and SRRI risk indicator
  • Composite Efficiency Labels: Identifies the cost leader, scale leader, and balanced funds across the set
  • Narrative Generation: Automated interpretation of the central finding (cost leadership vs scale leadership)
  • Replication Method: Differentiates between Physical and Synthetic (swap-based) structures
  • Source Traceability: Every fund links directly to its webpage, KIID, and factsheet for easy fact-checking
  • Interactive Dashboard: Self-contained HTML report with three interactive charts, KPI tiles, and styled tables

📉 Macro Market Regime Analysis Framework

Project Overview A historical analysis engine that classifies market environments (2000 to 2024) into 10 distinct regimes, from the dot com bubble to inflation shock, and evaluates how five major asset classes performed in each. It calculates regime specific Sharpe ratios, correlation shifts and optimal tactical allocations. Value Proposition Provides regime aware asset allocation insights through historical pattern analysis. Transforms complex market cycles into actionable strategic positioning. Technical Skills Showcases advanced quantitative analysis and time series segmentation. Validates expertise in statistical analysis and institutional grade data visualisation.
🔧 Core Analytics:
  • Regime-Specific Performance: Annualised returns, volatility, and Sharpe ratios across 10 market regimes
  • Multi-Asset Correlation Analysis: Dynamic correlation matrices showing relationship shifts across environments
  • Risk-Return Profiling: Visual scatter plots comparing volatility versus returns per asset class
  • Optimal Allocation Modelling: Historical and risk-adjusted portfolio suggestions per market condition
  • Comparative Regime Analysis: Side-by-side benchmarking of asset performance (2000-2024)
🌍 Sector & Market Analysis:
  • Five Asset Classes: US equities, global equities, bonds, gold, and US dollar analysis
  • Ten Historical Regimes: Dot-com Bubble through Inflation Shock (2000-2024)
  • Crisis vs Growth Analysis: Performance patterns during stress versus expansion periods
  • Safe Haven Behaviour: Study of bonds and gold as stabilisers during market declines
  • Recovery Dynamics: Analysis of leadership rotations during market rebounds
