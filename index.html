📊 AI Market Liquidity System

Project Overview A professional analytical platform that evaluates market liquidity conditions for any trading day since 2000. It analyses 60+ assets across equities, bonds, forex, commodities and crypto, calculating spreads, volume ratios and simulated depth, then forecasts spread movements using machine learning and simulates portfolio stress scenarios. Value Proposition Delivers executable market intelligence through systematic liquidity analysis. Transforms raw data into quantifiable liquidity signals that create actionable execution advantages. Technical Skills Demonstrates end to end financial data engineering and predictive modeling. Validates proficiency in building analytical pipelines from data ingestion to dashboard deployment.
🔧 Complete Feature Set:
  • Multi-Asset Analysis: 60+ assets across 8 categories
  • Date-Flexible Processing: Any trading date from 2000-present
  • Liquidity Metrics: Spread percentages, volume ratios, simulated depth

  • Sector Rotation Analysis: Heatmap rankings of 11 major ETFs
  • Yield Curve Monitoring: Treasury yield analysis

📈 ETF Cost Intelligence Engine

Project Overview A competitive intelligence engine comparing the S&P 500 UCITS ETF across six of Europe's largest providers (BlackRock, State Street, Vanguard, Xtrackers, Amundi, Invesco). It aggregates and ranks ongoing charges, fund scale, holdings count, and replication methods to reveal the true cost of passive investing. Value Proposition Delivers actionable product intelligence for ETF sales and capital markets desks. Transforms scattered regulatory data into a unified competitive landscape. Technical Skills Demonstrates deep ETF product knowledge (share class selection, physical vs synthetic replication) combined with structured data engineering.
🔧 Complete Feature Set:
  • Six-Provider Comparison: Deep-dive across top European issuers
  • Cost and Scale Metrics: TER, AUM, holdings, and SRRI risk indicator
  • Composite Efficiency Labels: Identifies cost leaders and scale leaders
  • Narrative Generation: Automated interpretation of the data
  • Replication Method: Physical vs Synthetic structures
  • Source Traceability: Links to KIIDs and factsheets
  • Interactive Dashboard: Self-contained HTML report
